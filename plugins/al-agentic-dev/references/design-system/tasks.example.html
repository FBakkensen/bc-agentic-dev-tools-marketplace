Sales Document Posting: Item Charge Allocation Validation

Slugsales-charge-validation
ADRADR-0007
Event modelevent-model.html
Architecturearchitecture.html
Tasks5 technical + 2 verify
Slicespost-validates-allocation, audit-trail
Goal

Catch item charge allocation mismatches at posting time before the invoice posts, surface the cause inline on the document, and produce a deterministic audit trail tying each allocation back to its source line.

Task bus

T-001Read released sales order item charge assignments

Resolve all Item Charge Assignment (Sales) rows for a released Sales Header, grouped by source Sales Line. Reads only; no Insert or Modify.

Scenarios

01 Reading assignments on a released order with one charge
Given a released Sales Header with No. "SO-1001" And one Item Charge Assignment (Sales) against a freight charge line When Charge Validation reads assignments for "SO-1001" Then one assignment is returned And it carries the source Sales Line reference
02 Reading assignments on an order with no charges yields empty
Given a released Sales Header with no Item Charge Assignment (Sales) rows When Charge Validation reads assignments Then the result is empty And validation is short-circuited as balanced
T-002Validate that allocated quantities sum to charge quantity

For each Item Charge Assignment (Sales), verify that the sum of allocated quantities across receiving Sales Line rows equals the charge quantity. Both inequality directions count as mismatches.

↻

Replan trigger fired during refine

Original test only covered equality, refined to cover both inequality directions. Coverage now: sum equals, sum less than, sum greater than.

Scenarios

01 Sum equals charge quantity
Given a charge of quantity 10 And allocations of 4 and 6 across two Sales Line rows When Charge Validation validates Then the result is balanced
02 Sum less than charge quantity
Given a charge of quantity 10 And allocations of 4 and 4 across two Sales Line rows When Charge Validation validates Then the result is Mismatch with shortfall 2
03 Sum greater than charge quantity
Given a charge of quantity 10 And allocations of 6 and 6 across two Sales Line rows When Charge Validation validates Then the result is Mismatch with overflow 2
T-003Subscribe to OnAfterCheckSalesDoc and route through validator

Add an event subscriber to Sales-Post codeunit 80 that delegates allocation validation to Charge Validation and propagates failures as posting errors. The subscriber must be defensive: a mismatch raises Error before any Insert or Modify on posting tables.

Depends on T-001 T-002

Scenarios

01 Validation fires before posting writes
Given a released Sales Header with one valid Item Charge Assignment (Sales) When the user calls Post Then Charge Validation runs before OnBeforePostSalesDoc And the Posted Sales Invoice is created with Posting Date matching the header
02 Mismatch aborts posting with no partial writes
Given a released Sales Header with a mismatched Item Charge Assignment (Sales) When the user calls Post Then posting halts with Allocation Mismatch And no Posted Sales Invoice row is Inserted
T-004Surface validation failure inline on Sales Order Card with allocation breakdown

When validation reports mismatch, render the allocation breakdown inline on the Sales Order Card — one row per receiving Sales Line, with allocated and required quantities side by side and the imbalance highlighted.

Depends on T-003

Scenarios

01 Breakdown shows allocated vs required per line
Given a mismatched assignment with allocations 6 and 6 against a charge of 10 When the user views the Sales Order Card after a failed Post Then the breakdown lists both Sales Line rows with allocated and required quantities And the overflow row is marked as the imbalance
02 Breakdown clears when the user corrects the allocation
Given a corrected allocation that sums to the charge quantity When the user re-opens the Sales Order Card Then the breakdown is hidden And the Validate action reports balanced
03 Breakdown survives navigation away and back
Given a failed posting on "SO-1042" with a persisted mismatch When the user navigates to another card and back to "SO-1042" Then the breakdown is still rendered And the most recent mismatch timestamp is shown
T-006Verify: Order Processor posts a sales document with a charge allocation and sees the mismatch surfaced inline

User-facing slice post-validates-allocation: the Order Processor releases and posts a Sales Header with an Item Charge Assignment (Sales); balanced allocations post cleanly, mismatched allocations halt posting with the inline breakdown on the Sales Order Card.

Depends on T-001 T-002 T-003 T-004

User test plan

01 Posting a balanced allocation succeeds end-to-end
1. Open Sales Order Card for SO-1041 with a charge of quantity 10 and allocations of 4 and 6 2. Click Post on the action bar 3. Confirm the posting dialog 4. Expected: a Posted Sales Invoice is created and the Sales Order Status flips to Released
02 Mismatched allocation halts posting with breakdown
1. Open Sales Order Card for SO-1042 with a charge of quantity 10 and allocations of 6 and 6 2. Click Post 3. Expected: posting halts with Allocation Mismatch message 4. Expected: the breakdown shows both Sales Line rows with allocated and required quantities, overflow row highlighted
03 Boundary at exact zero allocation
1. Open Sales Order Card for SO-1043 with a charge of quantity 10 and no allocations 2. Click Post 3. Expected: posting halts with Allocation Mismatch shortfall 10 4. Expected: the breakdown is rendered with zero allocated
T-005Persist allocation audit entries via Allocation Ledger Entry table

On every successful posting, Insert one ledger entry per resolved allocation: source Sales Line, receiving Sales Line, allocated quantity, Posting Date. The entry is the audit trail referenced by ADR-0007.
